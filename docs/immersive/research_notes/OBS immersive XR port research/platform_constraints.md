## KQ1 — visionOS: can libobs-style capture run on the headset (screen, camera, mic, Persona, spatial video, system audio), and what is App Store vs enterprise?

### Takeaway
On visionOS the only capture sources available to an App Store app are: the app's own rendered content, ReplayKit broadcast of the system UI with a BLACK background (no passthrough), the microphone, and the "Persona" front-camera device via AVFoundation (community-reported, not documented). Everything that makes OBS useful on a desktop (other apps' windows, the real cameras, the wearer's composite view) is gated behind Enterprise APIs that cannot be distributed through the App Store; there is no ScreenCaptureKit, no CoreMediaIO, and no system-audio capture.

### Cited Findings
- Enterprise API list (visionOS): `com.apple.developer.arkit.main-camera-access.allow` (forward-facing main camera), `com.apple.developer.arkit.camera-region.allow`, `com.apple.developer.arkit.shared-coordinate-space.allow`, `com.apple.developer.screen-capture.include-passthrough` ("Access a composite feed of what an Apple Vision Pro wearer is seeing (physical world and digital content)"), `com.apple.developer.arkit.barcode-detection.allow`, `com.apple.developer.protected-content`, `com.apple.developer.arkit.object-tracking-parameter-adjustment.allow`, `com.apple.developer.app-compute-category` ("increased power of the CPU and GPU ... with a tradeoff of increased thermal usage and reduced battery life"), `com.apple.developer.window-body-follow`, `com.apple.developer.arkit.visual-fidelity.allow` — [Apple: Building spatial experiences for business apps with enterprise APIs](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/visionos/building-spatial-experiences-for-business-apps-with-enterprise-apis)
- Eligibility: "Enterprise APIs for visionOS are eligible for business use only. You can distribute apps that you develop with the enterprise APIs for visionOS privately as proprietary in-house apps or custom apps using Apple Business Manager." Individuals enrolled as individuals cannot request a license; the Account Holder of an Apple Developer Program or Apple Developer Enterprise Program membership requests it. A "Development Only" license exists at team level for test devices. The license file has an expiration date and must be renewed — [Apple enterprise APIs doc](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/visionos/building-spatial-experiences-for-business-apps-with-enterprise-apis)
- WWDC24 statement: enterprise APIs are "only available for proprietary In-House apps or those custom-made for other businesses, privately distributed through Apple Business Manager"; visionOS 2.0+ — [WWDC24 10139](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2024/10139/) (excerpt only), [WWDCNotes summary](https://wwdcnotes.com/documentation/wwdc24-10139-introducing-enterprise-apis-for-visionos/) (excerpt only)
- Main camera: "a stereo camera that consists of left and right cameras. A person can configure the app to display the left, right, or combined stereo frames, with or without rectification, at one of two resolutions." Requires `NSMainCameraUsageDescription`. "Prior to visionOS 26, CameraFrameProvider is only available in an immersive space." Sample availability: visionOS 26.0+, Xcode 26.0+ — [Apple: Accessing the main camera](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/visionos/accessing-the-main-camera)
- WWDC25: main camera now gives "direct access to the left or right camera individually, or both for stereo processing and analysis", usable "in both Immersive Space and Shared Space". New `VisionEntitlementServices` framework (`EnterpriseLicenseDetails.shared.isApproved(for: .mainCameraAccess)`). "UVC video access and Neural Engine access are now available to all developers without an enterprise license or entitlement" — [WWDC25 223](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/223/)
- Screen capture of the wearer's view: Apple engineer on the forums — ReplayKit broadcast via a Broadcast Upload Extension is "available without the EnterpriseKit entitlement, but without the entitlement the frames captured contain a black background instead of passthrough"; the needed entitlement is `com.apple.developer.screen-capture.include-passthrough`, and it must be present on the broadcast extension target — [Apple Forums 757738](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/757738), [Apple Forums 815164](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/815164), [Apple Forums 809458](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/809458)
- Persona: Apple engineer states "there is no API to access the spatial persona" outside SharePlay; a separate community thread claims the front "Persona" camera is exposed as an AVFoundation capture device without an enterprise API; LiveKit's visionOS notes say camera switching is "limited to the Persona camera" and screen sharing is "more limited than on iOS/macOS" — [Apple Forums 766325](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/766325), [Apple Forums 749810](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/749810), [LiveKit Swift SDK visionOS page](https://mintlify.com/livekit/client-sdk-swift/platforms/visionos)
- Frameworks absent from the visionOS SDK include CoreMediaIO (virtual cameras), OpenGLES, OpenAL, WidgetKit, ActivityKit, NearbyInteraction — [Apple: Bringing your existing apps to visionOS](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/visionos/bringing-your-app-to-visionos)
- Spatial video capture for third-party apps exists via AVFoundation (iPhone-first; visionOS 27 adds "High Quality Capture", "up to three minutes of unfoveated 4K footage without a Mac" — a system feature, not an app API) — [Apple Immersive media overview](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technologyoverviews/immersive-media.md), [Virtual Reality News on visionOS 27 beta 2](https://virtual.reality.news/news/visionos-27-beta-2-whats-live-confirmed-and-delayed-for-fall/), [WWDC26 287](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/287/)
- Enterprise `contentCaptureProtected()` (protected content) blocks screenshots, recordings, AirPlay and SharePlay of a view — relevant because it shows capture is system-mediated, not app-mediated — [WWDC25 223](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/223/)

### Inferences
- A visionOS build of libobs can realistically offer: media/file sources, network sources (SRT/RTMP/WebRTC/NDI receive), browser-ish sources, the app's own Metal/RealityKit output, mic input, and (undocumented) Persona camera. "Display capture" and "window capture" of other visionOS apps do not exist for App Store apps; the closest thing is ReplayKit broadcast (system-level, user-initiated, black background) feeding a Broadcast Upload Extension with its own process/memory limits.
- Enterprise distribution (in-house/ABM) unlocks main stereo camera, passthrough-composited screen capture, and extra compute headroom, but excludes public App Store sales. A dual SKU (App Store "monitor/control" app + enterprise "capture" app) is the only way to cover both.
- No system-audio capture API was found for visionOS (consistent with iOS); treat "desktop audio capture" as unavailable.

### Gaps
- Exact main-camera resolutions/frame rates ("one of two resolutions") were not found in reachable docs; a forum post says "two resolutions" but the values were cut off — [Apple Forums 762506](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/762506).
- No official Apple documentation of the Persona AVFoundation device for third-party use was found; it rests on forum/SDK vendor claims.
- Broadcast Upload Extension memory limits on visionOS were not found (iOS limit is commonly cited as ~50 MB; not verified for visionOS).

## KQ2 — visionOS: networking, background execution, sustained hardware encoding, thermal/power

### Takeaway
visionOS inherits iOS background rules: an app that is not in the foreground is suspended unless it holds a qualifying background mode (audio etc.), and network traffic cannot wake it. Hardware HEVC/H.264 encoding via VideoToolbox exists on Vision Pro, but sustained encode budgets and thermal behavior are undocumented; Apple's own "increased performance headroom" entitlement explicitly warns of thermal/battery tradeoffs. M5 Vision Pro battery is 2.5 h general use.

### Cited Findings
- Apple engineer: "visionOS, like all Apple platforms other than macOS, puts strict limits on what your app can do in the background"; you "won't be able to resume your app in the background based on MQTT traffic"; the iOS Background Execution Limits discussion applies — [Apple Forums 751495](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/751495)
- Apple: an app "is typically suspended when it's in the background, but there are a limited number of background execution modes that enable it to run, such as playing audio, receiving location updates, or processing scheduled tasks" — [Apple: Configuring background execution modes](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/configuring-background-execution-modes.md)
- WWDC25 "Finish tasks in the background" introduces new iOS/iPadOS 26 APIs for finishing work on foreground-to-background transition; applicability to visionOS not stated — [WWDC25 227](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/227/)
- Enterprise entitlement `com.apple.developer.app-compute-category`: "Use increased power of the CPU and GPU for high-compute needs, with a tradeoff of increased thermal usage and reduced battery life" — [Apple enterprise APIs doc](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/visionos/building-spatial-experiences-for-business-apps-with-enterprise-apis)
- VideoToolbox overview lists Vision Pro among devices exposing dedicated encoder/decoder silicon; HEVC is the preferred hardware codec; AV1 hardware encode "not yet broadly available on Apple Silicon as of 2026" — [Forasoft VideoToolbox glossary](https://www.forasoft.com/learn/video-encoding/glossary/terms/videotoolbox) (vendor source)
- Apple's low-latency encode mode (WWDC21) "always uses a hardware-accelerated video encoder" and covers H.264 on iOS/macOS — [WWDC21 10158](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2021/10158)
- M5 Vision Pro: 10-core CPU, 10-core GPU with hardware ray tracing, 16-core Neural Engine, 10% more rendered pixels, up to 120 Hz, battery "up to 2.5 hours of general use (and up to 3 hours of video)", 16 GB unified memory, Wi‑Fi 6 (802.11ax), Bluetooth 5.3, refresh rates 90/96/100/120 Hz — [Auganix](https://www.auganix.org/?p=17722), [MacTech](https://www.mactech.com/2025/10/15/apple-vision-pro-updated-with-an-m5-chip-and-dual-knit-band/), [Apple support tech specs 125436](https://support.apple.com/en-us/125436) (excerpt only), [EveryMac](https://everymac.com/systems/apple/vision/specs/apple-vision-pro-m5-2025-specs.html) (excerpt only)
- visionOS 27 marketing: Vision Pro "connects to Wi-Fi up to three times faster" — [Apple visionOS 27 page](https://www.apple.com/os/visionos/) (excerpt only)
- Compositor Services render loop: "render up to 90 frames a second using a custom rendering loop" (doc wording, predating M5 120 Hz) — [Apple: Drawing fully immersive content using Metal](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/compositorservices/drawing-fully-immersive-content-using-metal)

### Inferences
- A visionOS libobs output (RTMP/SRT/WHIP) will stop when the user leaves the app for another app's window set or takes the headset off (device sleep), unless the app uses the audio background mode (likely acceptable for a streaming app that also plays/monitors audio; subject to review). "Looking away" inside the Shared Space while the window remains open does not background the app; opening another app's immersive space does hide your windows (see KQ4) and may trigger suspension.
- Vision Pro's Wi‑Fi 6 (no 6E/7) radio is a hard ceiling for any monitoring/return feed; plan ≤ 50–80 Mbps sustained and expect contention with Mac Virtual Display if both are active.
- Sustained 1080p60/4K HEVC encoding is technically available but the only documented lever for thermal headroom is an enterprise-only entitlement; expect throttling and ≈2–2.5 h battery when encoding plus immersive rendering.

### Gaps
- No published thermal-throttling measurements for Vision Pro under sustained encode were found; one unanswered forum question notes the official operating limit is 30 °C ambient.
- No visionOS-specific confirmation of hardware HEVC encode performance ceilings (max concurrent sessions, 4K60 real-time) was found.

## KQ3 — visionOS rendering options and third-party engines (incl. Qt), input modalities, iPad-compat mode

### Takeaway
Three build paths exist: SwiftUI/RealityKit (windows, volumes, immersive spaces), CompositorServices + Metal (custom engine, fully immersive only on-device, plus progressive since visionOS 26), or iPad-compatibility mode (flat window). Qt Quick 3D Xr officially supports visionOS as its one non-OpenXR backend (Tier 1, must build Qt from source, Xcode deploy), but Qt's core visionOS QPA is immature: single QWindow, no multi-scene manifest. Input available to a custom engine: gaze+pinch via `onSpatialEvent`, ARKit hand tracking, keyboards/trackpads/gamepads via Game Controller framework, and (visionOS 26+) PSVR2 Sense controllers and Logitech Muse.

### Cited Findings
- Compositor Services: `ImmersiveSpace { CompositorLayer { layerRenderer in ... } }`; "Don't include any style modifiers on a space that contains a CompositorLayer type. The system automatically configures a space with CompositorLayer content as fully immersive"; layouts `.dedicated`/`.shared`/`.layered`; foveation via `rasterizationRateMap`; P3 color; render loop with `queryNextFrame`/`predictTiming`/`startUpdate`/`endUpdate`/`wait(until:)`/`startSubmission`/`queryDrawables`/`encodePresent`/`endSubmission`; device anchor from ARKit `ar_world_tracking_provider_query_device_anchor_at_timestamp`; input via `layerRenderer.onSpatialEvent` (main thread) or ARKit hand tracking; render loop must run on a dedicated thread; full C API (`cp_*`) — [Apple: Drawing fully immersive content using Metal](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/compositorservices/drawing-fully-immersive-content-using-metal)
- WWDC25 additions for Metal immersive apps: progressive immersion with CompositorLayer (`.layered` layout, stencil portal mask), hover effects via tracking-areas texture (8-bit = up to 255 interactive objects), dynamic render quality (`maxRenderQuality`, `renderQuality`, requires foveation), `queryDrawables()` returning 1–2 drawables (second for Reality Composer Pro capture); "Metal 3 remains supported" — [WWDC25 294](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/294/)
- WWDC26 (visionOS 27): "CompositorServices: connect a proprietary engine and render directly into an Immersive Space"; Unity (Pro license required; windowed via RealityKit; immersive via RealityKit or CompositorServices; PSVR2 plugin), Unreal (immersive mode, static foveation), Godot (CompositorServices rendering, RealityKit plugin, PHASE audio plugin, on GitHub); hand tracking at 90 Hz; "over 4K pixels per eye" — [WWDC26 287](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/287/)
- Apple's WWDC26 visionOS guide: developers "using their own engine can adopt the CompositorServices API directly — on both visionOS and macOS" — [Apple WWDC26 visionOS guide](https://developer.apple.com/wwdc26/guides/visionos/) (excerpt only)
- Godot: Apple engineers added visionOS flat-window support first, with immersive XR via plugin later; Godot export docs (unstable) still say only flat is supported — [Road to VR](https://roadtovr.com/?p=172858) (excerpt only), [UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/godot-now-supports-visionos-apple-vision-pro/) (excerpt only), [Godot docs](https://docs.godotengine.org/it/4.x/tutorials/export/exporting_for_visionos.html) (excerpt only)
- Qt Quick 3D Xr: "currently supports one non-OpenXR platform: Apple's visionOS"; Apple Vision Pro is a Tier One tested device (standalone, visionOS); "QtQuick3D.Xr is still under development, and all features might not be available on all platforms"; on visionOS depth submission "is implicit and cannot be disabled or controlled by the application" — [Qt 3D XR supported platforms](https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/qt3dxr-supported-platforms.html) (excerpt only), [XrView QML type](https://doc.qt.io/qt-6.10/qml-qtquick3d-xr-xrview.html) (excerpt only)
- Qt visionOS quick start: "there is no binary build of Qt for visionOS, so you will need to build Qt from source"; configure with `-platform macx-visionos-clang` and `-sdk xros` / `-sdk xrsimulator`, `-submodules qtquick3d`; requires Apple Silicon; "visionOS applications are not deployed using Qt Creator, but instead using Xcode"; "Low-immersion apps are essentially iPad/iOS apps running in a window. High-immersion apps run in 'full screen' mode, meaning QtQuick3D takes complete control of the rendering"; "visionOS supports applications transitioning between these two states, but this is not supported by QtQuick3D.Xr" — [Qt: Quick start guide Apple Vision Pro](https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/qt3dxr-quick-start-guide-applevisionpro.html) (excerpt only)
- Qt core (qtbase iOS QPA) visionOS history: initial commit "there is currently no way to bring up QWindows"; a later UIScene-lifecycle change says "QWindows can now be created and shown on visionOS", but the app "does not declare a UIApplicationSceneManifest ... or report UIApplicationSupportsMultipleScenes" — i.e., multi-window Qt on visionOS is not enabled — [qtbase ios platform log](https://code.qt.io/cgit/qt/qtbase.git/log/src/plugins/platforms/ios?showmsg=1) (excerpt only), [qiosviewcontroller.mm log](https://code.qt.io/cgit/qt/qtbase.git/log/src/plugins/platforms/ios/qiosviewcontroller.mm?showmsg=1) (excerpt only)
- iPad/iOS apps on visionOS: rebuild against the visionOS SDK or run as compatible apps; UIKit works but "you can't include visionOS-specific or 3D content without using SwiftUI"; list of frameworks missing from the visionOS SDK (CoreMediaIO, OpenGLES, WidgetKit, etc.) — [Apple: Bringing your existing apps to visionOS](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/visionos/bringing-your-app-to-visionos)
- Input accessories: visionOS 26 added PlayStation VR2 Sense controllers ("high-performance motion tracking in 6 degrees of freedom, finger touch detection, and vibration support") and Logitech Muse stylus; games may require spatial controllers and the whole visionOS UI can be navigated with them — [Apple Newsroom visionOS 26](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/06/visionos-26-introduces-powerful-new-spatial-experiences-for-apple-vision-pro/) (excerpt only), [MacStories visionOS 26 review](https://www.macstories.net/stories/visionos-26-the-macstories-review/8/) (excerpt only)
- visionOS 27 spatial accessories: custom accessories need LED constellation + IMU + Bluetooth; tracked at native refresh rate; connect via Game Controller framework; reference hardware from DFRobot and MikroE "later this year" — [WWDC26 287](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/287/)
- Bluetooth: Xbox/PlayStation/Switch controllers supported ("any controller that works with iPadOS"); Apple Magic Trackpad supported, third-party trackpads and Bluetooth mice reported NOT supported (MacRumors guide) vs Cult of Mac presenting trackpads generally — conflicting — [MacRumors guide](https://macrumors.com/guide/vision-pro-bluetooth-accessories) (excerpt only); [Cult of Mac](https://www.cultofmac.com/how-to/vision-pro-bluetooth-keyboard-trackpad-controller) (excerpt only)
- RemoteImmersiveSpace input: "Any input device connected to the Mac works, including keyboard and mouse"; pinch events via `onSpatialEvent` — [WWDC25 294](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/294/)

### Inferences
- For a Qt-based immersive UI, the viable native path is: Qt Quick 3D Xr "high-immersion" mode (which internally drives CompositorServices; Qt does not name CompositorServices in its docs but visionOS has no other Metal immersive API) for the 3D stage, with 2D panels rendered as textures inside the immersive scene — because Qt cannot currently open multiple native visionOS windows/volumes alongside the space, and mode switching between windowed and immersive is unsupported by Qt.
- To get native multi-window + volumes + immersive space simultaneously (the "OBS docks floating around me" design), the shell must be SwiftUI with Qt embedded (e.g., Qt rendering into Metal textures presented by RealityKit/SwiftUI), or Qt restricted to the immersive layer only.
- Gaze+pinch is the only guaranteed pointer; mouse support is unreliable, trackpad = Apple Magic Trackpad only. Design for pinch/hand + keyboard + optional PSVR2 Sense controllers.

### Gaps
- Qt's actual use of CompositorServices (API-level) was not confirmable because doc.qt.io and code.qt.io were unreachable; only doc excerpts were available.
- Whether Qt 6.10/6.11 added `UIApplicationSupportsMultipleScenes` support for visionOS was not confirmed.

## KQ4 — visionOS multi-window: can one app show several windows + a volume + an immersive space at once? Limits

### Takeaway
Yes: with `UIApplicationSupportsMultipleScenes=true`, an app "can display any number of windows together with an immersive space", but only ONE immersive space may be open system-wide at a time, and opening it hides every other app's windows (and vice versa). Volumes are windows with `.volumetric` style and count as windows.

### Cited Findings
- "To enable an iPadOS or visionOS app to simultaneously display multiple scenes — including ImmersiveSpace scenes in visionOS — add the UIApplicationSupportsMultipleScenes key with a value of true" — [Apple: Presenting windows and spaces](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/visionos/presenting-windows-and-spaces)
- "Because your app operates in a Full Space when you open an ImmersiveSpace scene, you can only open one scene of this type at a time. If you try to open a space when one is already open, the system logs a runtime error." — same source
- "Your app can display any number of windows together with an immersive space. However, when you open a space from your app, the system hides all windows that belong to other apps. After you dismiss your space, the other apps' windows reappear. Similarly, the system hides your app's windows if another app opens an immersive space." — same source
- You cannot programmatically dismiss your app's last open scene; open the new scene before closing the old one — same source
- `UIApplicationPreferredDefaultSceneSessionRole = UISceneSessionRoleImmersiveSpaceApplication` (or `CPSceneSessionRoleImmersiveSpaceApplication` for CompositorLayer) launches into the space — [Presenting windows and spaces](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/visionos/presenting-windows-and-spaces), [Drawing fully immersive content using Metal](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/compositorservices/drawing-fully-immersive-content-using-metal)
- Unity's description of Shared Space vs Full Space: "Shared Space is a multitasking mode where users can see and interact with Windows and Volumes from multiple applications at once"; "a Full Space only displays content from one app at a time" — [Unity visionOS manual](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.0/manual/platform-specific/vision-os.md)
- Enterprise-only `window-body-follow` entitlement enables "Window Follow Mode" for "any window on the device in all applications" — [WWDC25 223](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/223/)
- Apple did not publish a numeric cap on windows; `supportsMultipleWindows` environment value reports capability at runtime — [Presenting windows and spaces](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/visionos/presenting-windows-and-spaces)

### Inferences
- An OBS-style layout of N floating 2D panels (scenes, sources, mixer, preview, program) + a volumetric multiview + a mixed-immersion "stage" is permitted in one app; the practical cap is memory/GPU, not a documented count. Coexisting with a user's other apps (e.g., a chat client) is only possible while the OBS app does NOT hold an immersive space (mixed/progressive/full all count).
- A CompositorLayer space is always full immersion on-device (progressive possible since visionOS 26), so any passthrough-friendly "mixed" stage must be RealityKit-based, not Metal/Qt-rendered.

### Gaps
- No Apple document giving a hard maximum number of simultaneous windows/volumes was found.

## KQ5 — App Store review and GPL/LGPL (OBS GPLv2, VLC precedent, Qt LGPL static linking)

### Takeaway
OBS Studio is GPLv2; the FSF/VLC history shows GPLv2 code is considered incompatible with Apple's App Store terms by the people who enforced it, and VLC resolved it by relicensing to LGPLv2.1+ after securing permission from 230+ contributors. Qt under LGPL can ship on iOS/visionOS statically linked only if users can relink (object files + build scripts), which practitioners consider dubious on App Store devices; Qt's commercial license is the standard escape hatch. For OBS, App Store distribution would need either a relicense/dual-license from all libobs copyright holders or avoiding the App Store (enterprise/TestFlight/sideload do not exist on visionOS beyond enterprise/ABM).

### Cited Findings
- obs-studio `COPYING` is "GNU GENERAL PUBLIC LICENSE Version 2, June 1991" — [obs-studio COPYING](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/blob/master/COPYING)
- VLC iOS was removed after Rémi Denis-Courmont notified Apple in October 2010 that App Store distribution violated the GPL; Apple's terms "add a DRM layer to all App Store apps, which restricts users' ability to freely redistribute software" — [rew.it.com explainer](https://rew.it.com/why-was-vlc-removed-from-app-store) (secondary), [Fierce Network](https://www.fierce-network.com/developer/apple-removes-vlc-media-player-from-app-store) (excerpt only)
- VLC relicensed from GPLv2+ to LGPLv2.1+ "partly to make it compatible with gadget-vendor app stores such as Apple's"; Jean-Baptiste Kempf needed authorization from "more than 230 individual developers"; VLC returned to the App Store in 2013 under the new license (date per 2025 explainer; 2012 LWN said "no ETA") — [LWN 525718](https://lwn.net/Articles/525718/) (excerpt only), [MacTrast 2012](https://www.mactrast.com/2012/11/popular-video-player-vlc-could-return-to-the-app-store/amp/) (excerpt only)
- VLC's own legal analysis of GPL vs App Store terms (2010) — [vlc-devel mailing list](https://mailman.videolan.org/pipermail/vlc-devel/2010-November/076998.html) (unreachable; cited for the record)
- Pidgin devs: "any distribution via Apple's App Store is a direct violation of the GPL" — [Pidgin wiki](https://developer.pidgin.im/wiki/WhyNoiOSVersion%3Faction=diff&version=6.html) (excerpt only)
- Apple Developer Program License Agreement quoted on forums: "If Your Application includes any FOSS, You agree to comply with all applicable FOSS licensing terms" — [Apple Forums 722101](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/722101) (excerpt only)
- Qt LGPL on iOS: "the LGPL dictates that you must provide a way for end users to re-link with a different version of Qt. Generally you'd fulfill this license requirement by providing the necessary object files and Makefiles to rebuild your application"; attribution required; otherwise buy a commercial license — [Qt development list, July 2014](https://lists.qt-project.org/pipermail/development/2014-July/017532.html)
- Skepticism: "providing relinkable objects is a useless solution anyway, unless people jailbreak their Apple devices" (2018); "on iOS you only get static linking right now" — [Qt interest list, Aug 2018](https://lists.qt-project.org/pipermail/interest/2018-August/030647.html); [Qt forum](https://forum.qt.io/topic/84051/distribution-with-lgpl)
- OBS Project enforced its GPL against TikTok Live Studio (2021/2022) "in good faith" — shows the project actively polices license terms — [Open Source For U](https://www.opensourceforu.com/?p=53344) (excerpt only)

### Inferences
- A visionOS "OBS" that links libobs (GPLv2) cannot be sold on the App Store without a license grant from the OBS Project and all libobs contributors (or a from-scratch LGPL/MIT engine). Enterprise distribution avoids the App Store EULA problem but limits the audience to organizations.
- Qt should be used under a commercial license for any App Store build; LGPL static linking on visionOS is legally fragile.

### Gaps
- No statement from the OBS Project about App Store distribution or a license exception was found.
- FSF's 2010 GNU Go statement page was unreachable; the specific GPLv2 §6 argument is cited via secondary sources only.

## KQ7 — Streaming video to Vision Pro for monitoring: WebRTC, LL-HLS, NDI, and Apple Foveated Streaming (CloudXR)

### Takeaway
For program/preview monitoring on the headset: WebRTC (LiveKit Swift SDK supports visionOS 1.0+) gives sub-second (~200–500 ms vendor-typical, <200 ms best case) latency; LL-HLS via AVPlayer is 2–6 s; the NDI SDK's Apple build reportedly includes visionOS (community-verified, not confirmed on NDI's site). A fourth option arrived in visionOS 26.4 (2026-03-24): Apple's `FoveatedStreaming` framework streams an entire OpenXR app from a Windows PC with an NVIDIA RTX 40/50-series GPU via NVIDIA CloudXR 6.0 at full fidelity with eye-tracked foveated compression — which does not help a Mac host.

- A standalone Steam Frame build of the Qt immersive OBS is feasible as a Linux arm64 OpenXR (Vulkan preferred) app with libobs cross-compiled for aarch64 Linux — PipeWire/V4L2 capture and VA-API/Qualcomm encoders are the open question; this is a Linux porting task, not a platform-policy problem. No app-store review or GPL conflict exists (Steam's distribution terms are not the App Store's), and sideloading is officially supported.
- Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 + 16 GB is adequate for UI + decode; hardware HEVC/H.264 encode on-device via V4L2/Venus is plausible but unverified for SteamOS Arm.

### Gaps
- No reachable Valve document describing the on-device OpenXR runtime's exact extension set (e.g., `XR_KHR_vulkan_enable2`, eye-tracking extensions, passthrough) was found.
- Hardware video encoder availability on the Frame's SteamOS (Arm) for an on-device libobs output was not found.

## KQ11 — macOS + Steam Frame: can a Mac drive the Frame?

### Takeaway
No supported path. Valve ended SteamVR for macOS in May 2020 (legacy beta branch only), Valve's Steam Frame guide lists Windows as the only fully supported streaming host (Linux partial), and no Mac host support for the Frame was found anywhere. Only third-party viewers (e.g., "Stream Frame" to view/record the Frame's output on a Mac) exist, which is the opposite direction.

### Cited Findings
- SteamVR macOS discontinued (May 2020) "so our team can focus on Windows and Linux"; older builds available via a macOS beta branch — [AppleInsider](https://appleinsider.com/articles/20/05/01/valve-abandons-the-macos-version-of-steamvr), [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/streamvr-mac), [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/72244/valve-announces-the-end-of-steamvr-support-for-macos/index.html)
- Valve guide: Frame adapter streaming "currently available on Windows only" — [MIXED](https://mixed-news.com/en/valve-guide-steam-frame-adapter-streaming-windows-only/) (excerpt only)
- Steam Link on macOS is a client (receiving), requires macOS 10.13+ and another Windows/Mac/Linux host — [MacRumors 2021](https://www.macrumors.com/2021/03/23/steam-link-launching-on-mac/) (excerpt only); Mac in-home-streaming hosting was disabled on Mojave (2018 thread) — [Steam community](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/homestream/discussions/0/1743352529782198260) (excerpt only)
- Third-party Moonlight XR streams from Sunshine/Apollo hosts (Sunshine supports macOS, but this is flat game streaming, not SteamVR) — [awesome-steam-frame](https://github.com/kosantosbik/awesome-steam-frame)

### Inferences
- A Mac-hosted libobs can only reach a Steam Frame via generic network video (NDI/WebRTC/Moonlight/WebXR in the Frame's Chromium build) into a native or sideloaded viewer app on the Frame — no OpenXR runtime on the Mac side exists to render an immersive Qt UI for the Frame.

### Gaps
- No statement from Valve explicitly naming macOS as unsupported for the Frame was found; it is inferred from "Windows only".

## KQ12 — Debugging tooling relevant to a Qt app: visionOS Simulator, Reality Composer Pro, Developer Strap, device logs

### Takeaway
The Xcode visionOS Simulator cannot simulate hand tracking (mouse-driven gaze/pinch only) and may lack device-only CompositorServices capabilities; real-device testing is mandatory for hand input and foveation. The 2nd-generation Developer Strap (Oct 2025, $299, up to 20 Gb/s vs 480 Mb/s USB 2.0 on the original, works on M2 and M5) adds a wired network interface for faster installs/debugging; wireless Xcode debugging also works. Qt apps deploy via Xcode, not Qt Creator, on visionOS.

### Cited Findings
- Simulator: "there's currently no way of testing or simulating hand tracking in the Vision Pro Simulator" (2023 report; may be outdated); Apple guidance that hand tracking must be verified on device — [Varrall Substack](https://varrall.substack.com/p/hand-tracking-in-vision-pro-simulator) (excerpt only), [Mixed-news dev tools](https://mixed-news.com/en/developer-tools-available-for-the-apple-vision-pro/) (excerpt only)
- Compositor Services: "Configuration options that are available on a device might not be available in Simulator, so use the capabilities parameter to validate your choices" — [Apple: Drawing fully immersive content using Metal](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/compositorservices/drawing-fully-immersive-content-using-metal)
- Developer Strap (gen 1): USB 2.0, creates "a new wired network interface ... used by Xcode for pushing builds and debugging"; mainly speeds large installs; wireless debugging also possible — [Apple Forums 746794](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/746794), [Apple Forums 745849](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/745849)
- Developer Strap gen 2: ordered from 2025-10-22, $299, "up to 20 Gbps" (vs 480 Mbps), compatible with M2 and M5, Apple Developer Program members only — [heise](https://heise.de/-10794066) (excerpt only), [MacMagazine](https://macmagazine.com.br/post/2025/10/22/apple-lanca-nova-developer-strap-para-o-vision-pro-com-velocidade-de-transferencia-maior/) (excerpt only), [UploadVR](https://uploadvr.com/apple-developer-strap-upgrade) (excerpt only)
- Reality Composer Pro capture: `queryDrawables()` returns a second `.capture` drawable when recording high-quality video via Reality Composer Pro (Mac-tethered capture for 2D viewing) — [WWDC25 294](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/294/); visionOS 27 adds on-device "High Quality Capture" (4K, up to 3 min, no Mac) — [WWDC26 287](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/287/), [Virtual Reality News](https://virtual.reality.news/news/visionos-27-beta-2-whats-live-confirmed-and-delayed-for-fall/)
- Xcode Foveated Streaming instrument reports stream bandwidth, pose latency, frame rate — [WWDC26 286](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/286/)
- Qt: visionOS apps "are not deployed using Qt Creator, but instead using Xcode"; Qt must be built from source — [Qt quick start Apple Vision Pro](https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/qt3dxr-quick-start-guide-applevisionpro.html) (excerpt only)

### Inferences
- For a Qt/CompositorServices app, the Simulator is useful only for 2D/window layout and basic immersive rendering; foveation, hand tracking, PSVR2 controllers, enterprise camera, Foveated Streaming and RemoteImmersiveSpace all need hardware (and for RemoteImmersiveSpace, a Mac on macOS 26 plus a headset on visionOS 26).
- The gen-2 Developer Strap (20 Gb/s) is worth budgeting ($299) for fast iteration on large Qt/libobs bundles; it does not change runtime networking for streaming features.

### Gaps
- Whether Apple's visionOS Simulator supports CompositorServices progressive immersion and RemoteImmersiveSpace targets was not confirmed.
- Whether the gen-2 strap's wired network interface can be used for app-level networking (e.g., NDI over USB) was not found.
